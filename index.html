Mission

To inform, enable, and inspire a sustainable future.

The Future Map exists because the climate crisis is not a distant problem, it's happening now at an unprecedented pace. Our future demands understanding and action, and the map is being built to show you where things are, what is being done, who is doing it, and how you can be a part of the change.

The Foundations

Inform

Clear, accurate, and regularly updated information on clean energy development and climate impact across every region of the world.

Enable

Resources and connections to organizations working on the ground, so anyone can turn awareness and understanding into real action.

Inspire

Stories of progress, remarkable people, and the case that the future is not set in stone, and that action by anyone at every scale makes a difference.
